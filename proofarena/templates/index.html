What is enforced

  • At most one approved winner per bounty.
  • Payout and refund are mutually exclusive and terminal.
  • A submitted transfer shows PayoutPending, never Paid.
  • An ambiguous transfer freezes into NeedsReconciliation; no blind retry.
  • Agent tokens are scoped to one challenge and one role.

What is not claimed

  • No NEAR testnet deployment in this build (BLOCKED: no credentials/network).
  • Reports are evidence for a human decision, not a proof of security.
  • The verifier is a single operator, not a trustless quorum.
  • No mainnet, no real funds, no token.
